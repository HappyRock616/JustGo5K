# Just Go 5K — Privacy

Just Go 5K does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

**What the app stores, and where**
- Your training progress (plan start date and a summary of each run: date, run/walk/pause time, distance, calories, heart rate average/max, latest SpO2 reading if available) and simplified routes for this week's runs (for the pace map) are stored **only on your watch**, in the app's own storage. Routes from earlier weeks are deleted automatically.
- Your settings (theme, units, alerts, dim screen) are stored on your watch and can be edited in the Garmin Connect app.

**Activities**
- Each run is saved as a standard Garmin activity on your watch. It syncs through Garmin Connect, or another app you choose such as Gadgetbridge, exactly like your other activities, under those services' own privacy terms. Just Go 5K itself never sends data anywhere.

**Sensors and permissions**
- GPS (outdoor runs only), heart rate and SpO2 history are read on the watch to coach you and record the activity.
- The app has no internet access, no analytics, no ads and no accounts.

**Deleting your data**
- Settings → Restart plan clears all progress and routes stored by the app. Uninstalling the app removes its storage. Activities already saved to Garmin Connect are managed there.
