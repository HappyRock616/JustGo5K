## Description

From your first jog to running 30 minutes without stopping, in 9 weeks — coached entirely from your watch. No phone needed during the run.

**How it works**
• 3 runs a week for 9 weeks, starting with 1-minute jogs and building to a 30-minute run.
• Every session starts with a 5-minute warm-up walk and ends with a 5-minute cool-down.
• The screen switches automatically between run and walk. You never have to touch the watch mid-run.
• A 3-2-1 countdown with distinct vibrations (and optional tones) tells you when to run and when to walk.

**Stay on track without a fixed schedule**
• Weeks run Sunday to Saturday, and you pick your own run days.
• The home screen shows your week at a glance: run days ticked, rest days marked with a sleeping-on-the-sofa icon, and how many runs and days you have left.
• A gentle "go today" nudge appears when you're running out of week.
• Finish all 3 runs and the next week unlocks on Sunday. Miss some and the plan still moves on, so there's no falling behind.
• Starting mid-week? Do bonus warm-up runs; week 1 begins on the following Sunday.

**See your progress**
• Run history chart: stacked run/walk time for every run, with calories on top and missed runs marked.
• Stats table: distance, pace, heart rate average and max, calories, and your watch's latest SpO2 reading where supported.
• Pace map: this week's routes coloured by pace on a fixed scale (blue walking through to red sprinting), so every run reads the same way. Indoor runs show a pace ring instead. Every route is also kept in full in its Garmin activity.
• A graduation screen compares your first run with your last.

**Indoor or outdoor**
Choose Start Outdoor (GPS) or Start Indoor (treadmill, pace from wrist motion) each time you run.

**Works with your apps**
Every run is saved as a normal Garmin running activity, with one lap per run/walk interval. It syncs to Garmin Connect, and from there to Strava and other services, just like your other runs. Warm-up and cool-down are kept out of the activity so your stats reflect the real workout.

**Make it yours**
5 colour themes · units follow your watch (or choose miles/km) · vibration only or vibration plus tone · optional dim screen during runs. Settings can be changed on the watch or in the Garmin Connect app.

**Controls**
Press Start to open today's run, then Start again to go. During a run, Start or Back pauses. Holds are needed to discard a run, redo a run or restart the plan, so nothing is lost by accident.

Before starting any exercise programme, check with your doctor if you have any health concerns.
